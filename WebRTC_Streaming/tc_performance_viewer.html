Understanding the Graphs

These graphs compare the commanded network conditions (what was set by the traffic control script) with the actual measured performance at the receiver.

The blue lines represent what was commanded by the traffic control (tc) system, while the red lines show what was actually measured during video streaming.

The correlation graphs show how well the commanded values match the measured values. Points on or near the dashed line indicate good correlation.
